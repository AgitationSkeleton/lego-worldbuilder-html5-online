**New units: the game's cut Bluebird and Cargo Copter, made whole, and an Airport.**

- **Bluebird**: the animal the game has a script and config for but never finished. It flies over
  land and water. Recipe as in the game's own config: 5 blue bricks and an energy brick.
- **Cargo Copter**: a flying carrier for up to 15 bricks, over land and water. Recipe: 20 blue,
  10 white and an energy brick. It recharges at the Airport.
- **Airport**: the building the game has a script for and nothing else, built after the airport
  of LEGO set 4028, the set the Cargo Copter comes from. It recharges the Cargo Copter.
- The flyers hover over their shadow on the map and sway gently, as the goal arrow does.
- All three are rendered from LEGO models in the style of the game's own pictures: their map
  sprites, info panel portraits and plan icons
  ([the renderer](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legoworldbuilder_renderer)). The Bluebird
  and Cargo Copter are from [JappaWakka](https://github.com/JappaWakka)'s recreations.

**Level editor**: the Bluebird, the Cargo Copter and the Airport can be placed, planned and set as
goals.

**Random missions: Rando v5**, the default generator now (codes with 8 characters after the dash).
- The new units can turn up as goals and bonus goals: build an Airport, bring a Bluebird or a
  Cargo Copter somewhere, fly one to a goal on the water. A Cargo Copter running low on a trip
  recharges at an Airport, built beside it if there is none.
- The ground between a map's parts is mostly sky again, as in the game's own worlds, instead of
  ranges of mountains (the Grassland and Ocean looks' especially, and no volcano fields in
  Prehistoric).
- Codes from Rando v1 to v4 still make the missions they always made.

**Settings > Pictures > Consistent Info Panel Portraits**: the game's info panel shows the Buggy and
the Dirtbuggy as other builds than the ones on the map, and the Freighter as the Tugboat. With this
on, they show the map's own models instead.
